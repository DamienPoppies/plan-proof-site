About

Small tools, plainly made.

Most business owners now know AI should be doing more than writing emails. Very few have anyone who will sit down, find the right job, and build the thing.

That is the whole reason this exists. Not because AI is exciting — because there is a gap between “I have seen what this can do” and “there is a tool in my business that saves me a day a week”, and almost nobody is working in that gap.

The work is deliberately small. One job, one tool, one price, in weeks rather than quarters. That is not modesty; it is the only way to find out whether something is useful before anyone spends serious money on it.

It also means the answer is sometimes no. A job that happens twice a year does not deserve a tool. A process that is broken because two people disagree does not get fixed by software. Saying that early is part of the service, not a failure of the sales pitch.

How it behaves

Four habits

Plain language, always

If a sentence needs a glossary, it is the wrong sentence. The people who use the tool should be able to explain it to someone else.

Small enough to finish

Every piece of work has a defined end. Long projects are usually several small projects pretending to be one.

Honest about evidence

No invented case studies, no borrowed logos, no percentages that cannot be checked. If something is an example, it says so.

Yours at the end

Your accounts, your data, your code, documented well enough that another person could pick it up.

Who does the work

Onshore lead. Offshore build team.

Scoping and sign-off

Done here, in Australia, by the person you speak to. Nothing reaches you that has not been reviewed against the scope you signed.

Build

A small team we work with directly. They build to the written scope; they do not decide what gets built, and they do not talk to your customers.

Your data

Client data stays in Australian accounts you control. The build team works against test data, or through access you can revoke at any time.

Ownership

Code, data and documentation transfer to you on final payment. No licence, no dependency on us to keep it running.

Accountability

One person answers for the result. If the build misses the acceptance test, that is our problem to fix, not yours to negotiate.

Boundaries

What this is not

  • Not an AI consultancy No strategy decks, no maturity models, no transformation roadmaps.
  • Not a software agency No twenty-page specifications and no six-month builds.
  • Not a reseller Nothing here depends on a licence you have to keep buying from us.
  • Not a big-bang build A first version earns the next one. Bigger work waits until the first job is useful.
