Beyond one trade

If it happens every week, it can probably be a tool

Trades are where most of these conversations start, not where they end. The same problem turns up in finance, professional services and multi-site operations: a number nobody can see, a follow-up nobody owns, a policy that only exists as a PDF from 2019. A few of the shapes it takes:

Money in and out

  • Quoting and estimating from past jobs
  • Progress claims, variations and retentions
  • Invoices that chase themselves, politely
  • Cashflow you can see twelve weeks out
  • Job costing and margin, readable on a Friday

Demand and follow-up

  • Enquiries qualified before they reach you
  • Quote follow-up on a schedule, not a mood
  • Rebooking, reminders and review requests
  • Channel reporting that says which one worked
  • One piece of content, reused without rewriting

Paperwork, policy and proof

  • Pre-start packs, inductions and site rules
  • Handover packs: as-builts, warranties, manuals
  • Policies and registers that stay current
  • Approvals, sign-offs and a clean audit trail
  • Onboarding: access, checklists, training

Numbers and decisions

  • The weekly numbers, written before you ask
  • Pipeline and utilisation in one view
  • Client profitability, not just revenue
  • Renewals, obligations and dates that matter
  • The report your bank or board keeps requesting

Most of these start as one job inside one business. When the same shape turns up three times, it stops being a custom build and becomes something we can set up faster. Nothing here is a promise of integrations, and nothing gets built before the plan says it earns its keep.

Straight answers

Common questions

What does it cost if I go ahead?

The call is free. The plan is $750 and comes off the build in full, so starting costs you nothing extra. A first version usually lands between $4,000 and $9,000; a tool several people use, or one that replaces a system, sits well above that. The plan fixes your number before anything starts, and a larger job is scoped separately.

Will it work with Xero, or whatever I already use?

Usually, yes. Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks, Stripe, Shopify, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and most job-management systems have documented APIs, so the tool can read and write where it needs to, with your permission. If something has no way in, we say that in the plan rather than after you have paid.

Do I have to do all of it at once?

No. Work is staged, and stage one is deliberately small: one job, priced on its own. When it is in daily use and paying for itself, we scope stage two — a second job, a second role, or the reporting on top. Each stage has its own fixed price and its own decision, so the total only grows when it has earned it.

How long does a build take?

Weeks, not months. The first version is deliberately small: one job, done properly, in daily use before we add anything else.

What if it is not worth building?

Then we say so on the call, and the plan tells you what to do instead — usually a change to the process that costs nothing. That still counts as a good session.

Do I need to understand AI?

No. You need to understand your own job, which you already do. We handle the rest and explain it without jargon.

Who actually builds it?

We do, working with a small team we deal with directly. One person is accountable to you for the result, and everything is handed over when it is finished.
